# MyHub Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 31, 2026**

MyHub is a personal productivity app for managing todos, notes, and links.

## Information stored on your device

Todos, notes, links, and preferences are stored in the app's local SQLite database on your device. MyHub does not require an account for these features. The developer does not receive this locally stored content.

## Optional Google Drive backup

If you connect a Google account, MyHub uses Google Sign-In to access its private Google Drive appDataFolder. MyHub may display your Google account name and email in settings. When you start a backup or restore, a compressed backup file containing your todos, notes, links, and metadata is stored in your Google Drive app data area. It is accessed through the Google Drive API and is not sent to a MyHub-operated server. Google's handling of your account and Drive data is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Google Drive backup is optional. Disconnecting Google Drive removes the local account connection but does not delete an existing backup. You can manage or delete Drive data through your Google Account settings.

## Export and import

When you export a backup, MyHub creates a file on your device and presents the operating system's share or save interface. You choose whether and where to save or share it. Imported files are read to validate and restore MyHub data to the local database.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

MyHub does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not operate a service that collects app content or account information.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until deleted or until you remove the app and its local data. You control Google Drive backups and exported copies.

## Children's privacy

MyHub is a general-purpose productivity app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change.
