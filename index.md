# MyHub — Your daily workspace

MyHub helps you keep everyday tasks, personal notes, and useful links together in one simple mobile app for iOS and Android.

## Stay organized

- **Todos:** Create tasks, set due dates and priorities, and track completion.
- **Notes:** Capture ideas and information, format content, and pin important notes.
- **Links:** Save useful websites with descriptions and mark favourites.

Your MyHub content is stored locally on your device. You can optionally connect Google Drive to back up and restore your data in your private Drive app data folder. MyHub does not operate a server that stores your content.
